Product design

One sample case study, from research to tested prototype: a mobile checkout redesign for an online fashion store.

Persona: Sara, 27

Shops on her phone during her commute. Wants to pay in under a minute and hates retyping details.

Problems found
  • Checkout has 5 steps
  • Forced account creation
  • Shipping cost shown only at the end
Goal

Fewer steps, no surprises, one-thumb use on a small screen.
