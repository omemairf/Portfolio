Overview

Small businesses and first-time sellers in Pakistan often struggle with ecommerce platforms because they require plugins, technical setup, hosting configuration, and complex dashboards. Many users had no previous experience creating websites or managing online stores.

Create a no-code ecommerce platform that allows users to launch stores quickly without developer support or technical knowledge.

WebXEcommerce is an ecommerce and website-building platform combining store creation, visual editing, theme customization, product management, and website tools within one system.

Context

  • Small business owners
  • Local Pakistani brands
  • First-time online sellers
  • Non-technical entrepreneurs

Most available solutions focused on advanced users and required additional setup, plugins, or external integrations. Platforms such as Shopify, WordPress, and similar tools created onboarding friction for beginners.

  • Complex onboarding flows
  • Plugin dependency
  • Technical setup requirements
  • Difficult dashboards
  • Fragmented store management
  • Limited technical knowledge among users

Process

Research focused on understanding onboarding barriers and ecommerce adoption challenges.

Activities included:

  • Review of Shopify, WordPress, Wix, and regional solutions
  • Interviews with merchants and local businesses
  • Journey mapping from signup to first published store
  • Validation of onboarding pain points

I worked on structuring multiple product areas including:
  • Store dashboard
  • Product management
  • Order workflows
  • Analytics sections
  • Theme customization
  • Website editor states

The objective was reducing complexity while keeping flexibility.

Testing focused mainly on onboarding and store setup flows. Validation areas included:
  • First store creation
  • Product publishing
  • Theme switching
  • Page editing interactions
  • Setup completion rate

Several decisions were made to reduce user friction:
  • Replace plugin dependency with built-in features
  • Introduce ready-made industry templates
  • Simplify dashboard navigation
  • Use drag-and-drop editing instead of configuration panels
  • Reduce steps during onboarding

Solution

Designed management environments for:

  • Store setup
  • Products
  • Orders
  • Themes
  • Analytics
  • Website editing

The dashboard prioritized clarity and low learning effort.

Created 5 built-in themes for different business types:
  • Fashion & Clothing
  • Food & Restaurants
  • Electronics
  • Beauty & Cosmetics
  • Local Retail

Each theme included responsive layouts and simplified customization.

Developed an animated drag-and-drop experience allowing users to:
  • Rearrange sections
  • Edit content visually
  • Configure pages live
  • Manage products without coding

The platform removed technical barriers by providing:
  • Built-in ecommerce tools
  • Visual editing
  • Plugin-free setup
  • Ready storefront templates
  • Simplified management flows

After implementation the platform showed improvements in onboarding and activation.

51% reduction in store setup time

46% lower onboarding drop-off

34% increase in completed seller activation

Additional impact:

  • Higher theme adoption
  • Faster product publishing
  • Lower support dependency
