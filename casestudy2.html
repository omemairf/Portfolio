Overview

A redesign project for one of Pakistan’s leading medical universities focused on improving information access, restructuring content, simplifying navigation, and supporting academic and healthcare services. The platform included 11 academic departments, each with independent content, pages, announcements, programs, and information requirements. The challenge was creating a consistent experience while maintaining department-level flexibility.

Create a structured digital experience that improves discoverability, supports institutional communication, and provides clearer access to academic and healthcare information.

A public university website covering:
  • Admissions
  • Institutes and departments
  • Academic information
  • Research sections
  • Student resources
  • Events and notices
  • Diagnostic laboratory access
  • Public healthcare services

Process

The work focused on understanding how institutional information should be organized and accessed.

Activities included:

  • Review of university websites and education portals
  • Analysis of admissions and academic journeys
  • Content grouping and hierarchy planning
  • Mapping high-priority pages and services

The redesign required organizing information across:
  • Admissions structure
  • 11 departmental structures
  • Department navigation
  • Research pages
  • Student services
  • Academic notices
  • Event updates
  • Diagnostic laboratory access
  • Contact and support areas

Each department maintained separate pages and content while following a unified system. The goal was balancing consistency, scalability, and information clarity.

Several decisions focused on clarity and accessibility:
  • Prioritized admissions visibility
  • Grouped academic resources by purpose
  • Reduced navigation depth
  • Highlighted notices and announcements
  • Improved access to healthcare and diagnostic services
  • Structured content for mobile users

Solution

Department Experience

Created a structured framework for 11 departments, allowing:

  • Individual department pages
  • Separate academic information
  • Program visibility
  • Faculty content organization
  • Department-specific announcements
  • Shared navigation patterns

Organized university content into clear navigation layers:

Academic Information → Departments → Services → Updates → Resources

This reduced content fragmentation.

The platform included visibility for diagnostic and laboratory services, allowing users to access healthcare information alongside university content.

JSMU diagnostic services include laboratory and blood bank functions together with multiple clinical areas.

Project outcomes focused on:

Improved content organization

Better access to admissions information

Clearer navigation structure

Reduced information fragmentation

Better visibility of healthcare services

The project focused on information architecture and institutional content management rather than visual redesign alone.
